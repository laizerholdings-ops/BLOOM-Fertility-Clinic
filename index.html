Urology & Andrology

Male Fertility Care & Analysis

Male factor infertility contributes to approximately half of all fertility challenges. Our specialized andrology evaluations include comprehensive semen analysis, hormone profiling, urological examination, and advanced sperm DNA fragmentation testing in Midrand.

Gynecology & IVF / IUI
